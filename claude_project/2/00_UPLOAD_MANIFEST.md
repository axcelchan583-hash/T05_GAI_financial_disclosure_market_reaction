## Review Goal

Please review this as a journal-targeted accounting / finance research design, not as a generic AI-washing idea.

The current design asks:

> Do specific GenAI capability claims remain informative when they lack prior publicly verifiable evidence?

The key concern from the research meeting is measurement credibility:

> The X and Y variables must not look like ad hoc measures invented by the authors. They need clear support from AJG 3/4 or equivalent mature literature.

## What To Evaluate

1. Is the research question clear and publishable?
2. Are `HighSpecificity`, `NoPriorPublicEvidence`, `AIHiringShare`, `AIPatentFiling`, and `FutureMatchedPublicEvidence` sufficiently anchored in prior literature?
3. Which measures still look self-constructed and should be downgraded to mechanism, validation, or robustness variables?
4. Should the main outcome order be:
   - future AI / GenAI hiring,
   - future AI / GenAI patents,
   - claim-level future matched public evidence?
5. What would a skeptical reviewer attack first?
6. Given the available data, should this design be continued, revised, or abandoned?
